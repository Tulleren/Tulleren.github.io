About the models

All three models were trained on the same field photos and tested on the same 785 test photos of the CD&S dataset. They know three maize leaf diseases: Gray Leaf Spot, Northern Leaf Blight and Northern Leaf Spot.

Limitations

  • The models only know these three diseases. Other diseases (for example common rust) are forced into one of them or, with spot detection, may give “No disease found”.
  • The two classifiers have no “healthy” answer: they always name a disease. Use spot detection to check whether a leaf looks healthy.
  • The training photos are close-ups of single leaves from fields in Indiana, USA. Photos of whole plants, other light or other regions work less well.
  • Results below the confidence threshold are marked uncertain and should be checked by eye.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your photos, videos and camera images are not uploaded anywhere.
