Welcome to the dashboard

In this site you’ll explore a complete social data visualization project built from the course assignments in Social Data Analysis and Visualization 2026.

You can inspect temporal trends, district crime profiles, category breakdowns, and spatial heatmaps that reveal hidden rhythms in San Francisco crime over 20+ years.

Each visualization is rooted in the lecture pipeline: data merge, cleaning, category filtering, focused crime analytics, and interactive time/spatial mapping.
