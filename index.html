About this tool

The model is an EfficientNet-B0 trained on the Corn Leaf Disease Classification Dataset. It recognises five classes:

  • Common Rust
  • Corn Leaf Blight
  • Gray Leaf Spot
  • Healthy
  • Insects damage

The model always picks one of these five classes, even for images that are not maize leaves. If its confidence is below 70%, the result is marked uncertain and should be checked manually.

Everything runs in your browser. Your images are not uploaded to any server.
